### 1. Product Goal

Enable small work teams to register, visualize, and update tasks through a simple, accessible web application that centralizes task information and facilitates day-to-day task tracking.

### 2. Problem It Solves

Small teams need a straightforward way to keep task information organized and up to date without the complexity of comprehensive project management tools.

### 3. Objective User

Small work teams and their members who need to manage and track tasks collaboratively through a simple interface.

### 4. Expected Value

Provide a lightweight, easy-to-use solution that improves task visibility, keeps task information organized, and reduces reliance on scattered or manual tracking methods.

### 5. Three Observable Indicators

1. Task registration: Users can create tasks and find them in the application after registration.

2. Task visibility: Users can view the registered tasks and identify their current information and status.

3. Task updates: Users can modify task information or status, and the changes remain available after reloading the application.
